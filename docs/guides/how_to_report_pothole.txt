How to Report a Pothole - City of Cape Town

Potholes should be reported so that the City can assess and repair them.

Official ways to report a pothole:

1. Online Service Request Portal:
   www.capetown.gov.za/servicerequests
   Select the category: Transport, Traffic and Roads

2. Call Centre:
   0860 103 089
   or
   0800 656 463 (Roads and Stormwater)

3. Email:
   Transport.Info@capetown.gov.za

4. City of Cape Town App (available on Apple App Store and Google Play)

Important details to provide:
- Exact street name and suburb
- Nearest street corner or landmark
- Description of the pothole (size if possible)

The City aims to assess and temporarily repair potholes as soon as possible after they are reported. Structural damage to the road may take longer to repair.

Responsible department: Urban Mobility (Roads Infrastructure Management)
