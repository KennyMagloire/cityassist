Important Tips when Logging a Service Request with the City of Cape Town

1. Always provide a clear location.
   Many service requests cannot be actioned without a proper location (street name, suburb, and nearest corner or landmark).

2. Be specific in your description.
   Instead of saying "there's a problem", describe exactly what you see (e.g. "burst water pipe with water flooding the road" or "large pothole near the intersection").

3. Keep your reference number.
   After logging a request, you will receive a reference number. Keep it so you can follow up later.

4. You can log requests online 24/7 at:
   www.capetown.gov.za/servicerequests

5. The City of Cape Town does not automatically know about problems. Residents need to report them.

6. For life-threatening emergencies, call 021 480 7700.
