How to Report an Electricity Fault - City of Cape Town

If you experience a power outage, damaged electrical equipment, or any electricity-related fault, you can report it using the following channels:

1. Online Service Request Portal:
   www.capetown.gov.za/servicerequests

2. Call Centre:
   0860 103 089 (Option 3 for Electricity)
   Available 24 hours a day

3. SMS:
   31220
   (Include your address, account or meter number if possible, and a short description)

4. Email:
   power@capetown.gov.za

What to include in your report:
- Physical address or location of the fault
- Description of the problem (no power, sparks, damaged cable, pole down, etc.)
- Meter number or account number (if available)
- Your contact number

Note: Some areas in Cape Town are supplied by Eskom. If you live in an Eskom supply area, you should contact Eskom on 0860 037 566.
