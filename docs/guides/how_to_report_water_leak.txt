How to Report a Water Leak - City of Cape Town

If you see a water leak (burst pipe, leaking valve, leaking meter, or water running in the street), you should report it as soon as possible.

Official ways to report a water-related fault:

1. Online Service Request Portal:
   www.capetown.gov.za/servicerequests

2. Call Centre:
   0860 103 089 (Option 2 for Water)
   Available 24 hours a day

3. SMS:
   31373
   (Include your address and a short description of the problem)

4. WhatsApp:
   060 018 1505

5. Email:
   water@capetown.gov.za

Important information to provide when reporting:
- Exact location (street name, suburb, nearest corner or landmark)
- Description of the problem (e.g. burst pipe, leaking meter, water running down the street)
- Your contact number

The City of Cape Town Water and Sanitation Department is responsible for water leaks on municipal infrastructure. Leaks inside private properties are usually the responsibility of the property owner.
