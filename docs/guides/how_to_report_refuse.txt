How to Report Uncollected Refuse or Request a New Bin - City of Cape Town

If your refuse was not collected, or you need a new wheelie bin, you can report it as follows:

1. Online Service Request Portal:
   www.capetown.gov.za/servicerequests

2. Call Centre:
   0860 103 089

3. Email:
   wastewise@capetown.gov.za

Common refuse-related requests:
- Non-collection of refuse
- Request for a new 240 litre wheelie bin
- Illegal dumping
- Overflowing public bins
- Garden refuse removal (in some areas)

When reporting, please provide:
- Your physical address
- Suburb
- Description of the problem
- Your contact number

Responsible department: Urban Waste Management
